Our Surveying Services

CityPlanners provides land and engineering survey support for property owners, developers, contractors, consultants and infrastructure project teams. Our survey services help establish site conditions and provide essential information for planning, design and construction.

Land Survey

Measurement and survey of land parcels, site boundaries, dimensions, area and physical site characteristics.

Topographic Survey

Mapping of terrain, ground levels, contours, natural features, structures and site details.

Construction Survey

Survey support for construction activities, reference points, alignment, positioning and level verification.

Site Survey

Existing site assessment and measurement for proposed developments, construction and infrastructure planning.

Contour Survey

Collection of elevation information and preparation of contour data for site planning and engineering purposes.

Road & Infrastructure Survey

Survey support for roads, highways, access roads, utilities and other infrastructure development.
