Airport Consultancy Services

CityPlanners provides transportation and infrastructure planning consultancy support for airport development and associated infrastructure projects. Our focus includes airport access, traffic circulation, landside transportation systems and infrastructure coordination.

Airport Transportation Planning

Transportation planning and assessment of passenger, employee, visitor and service vehicle movement requirements.

Airport Access Planning

Assessment of airport connectivity, access roads, entry and exit arrangements and external road networks.

Landside Traffic Planning

Planning and assessment of landside traffic circulation, vehicle movement, terminal frontage and access systems.

Terminal Traffic Circulation

Review of terminal frontage circulation, vehicle distribution, passenger drop-off and pick-up arrangements.

Airport Parking Planning

Parking demand assessment, parking layout review, circulation and access planning.

Airport Infrastructure Planning

Transportation and infrastructure planning coordination for airport development, expansion and associated works.
