BEFORE YOU PURCHASE OR LEASE LAND

What Should Be Checked?

Each proposed fuel station location is different. The assessment may consider the following information:

  • Exact site location and coordinates
  • Plot dimensions and area
  • Road frontage and access conditions
  • Road type and adjoining network
  • Existing traffic conditions
  • Nearby junctions and intersections
  • Median and service road conditions, where present
  • Adjacent properties and land use
  • Site visibility and circulation considerations
  • Applicable authority and road agency requirements
  • Available land records and drawings
  • Additional survey or technical information required

Don't Make a Land Investment Based on Plot Area Alone

Frontage, road characteristics, access conditions, site configuration and regulatory requirements can be important factors in fuel station site evaluation.


A project-specific assessment is recommended before making investment or development decisions.
