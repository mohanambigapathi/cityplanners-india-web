PROJECT APPLICATIONS

Where Traffic Management Is Required

Traffic management and diversion planning can support projects such as:

  • Road and highway construction
  • Road widening and rehabilitation
  • Metro rail and station construction
  • Flyover and bridge construction
  • Utility installation and underground works
  • Water supply and drainage projects
  • Building construction adjacent to roads
  • Urban infrastructure development
  • Temporary event and access management
  • Road maintenance and repair activities

Keep Traffic Moving During Construction

Construction activities can create congestion, access restrictions, conflicts and safety risks.


Effective traffic planning considers construction requirements together with road users, pedestrians, accessibility, surrounding roads and site conditions.

OUR APPROACH

Traffic Management Planning Process

Each project is assessed according to the construction scope, road conditions, traffic characteristics and required deliverables.

STEP 01

Project Review

Understand construction activities, project location, work programme, road conditions and constraints.

STEP 02

Site & Traffic Assessment

Review existing traffic, site access, affected road users, surrounding routes and available information.

STEP 03

Traffic Arrangement

Develop proposed traffic staging, diversion routes, work-zone arrangements and traffic control concepts.

STEP 04

Plan & Documentation

Prepare the agreed traffic management documentation, drawings, recommendations and supporting information.
