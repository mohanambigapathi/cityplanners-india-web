Building & Development Services

CityPlanners supports building owners, developers, commercial establishments and project teams with development planning, transportation assessment, access planning and infrastructure coordination.

Site Development Planning

Review of site conditions, land utilisation, development requirements and planning considerations.

Access & Connectivity

Assessment of site access, entry and exit arrangements, road connectivity and circulation requirements.

Parking Planning

Parking demand assessment, circulation, parking layout review and access arrangements.

Traffic Impact Assessment
