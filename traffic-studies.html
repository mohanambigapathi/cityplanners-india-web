OUR APPROACH

How We Undertake a Traffic Study

Our work is structured to understand project requirements, collect relevant data and develop practical findings.

STEP 01

Project Understanding

Review project location, development details, objectives and study requirements.

STEP 02

Survey Planning

Identify survey locations, data requirements, survey periods and collection methodology.

STEP 03

Data Collection & Analysis

Collect traffic data and undertake relevant engineering analysis.

STEP 04

Report & Recommendations

Present findings, assessment, conclusions and recommendations in a suitable report.

PROJECT-SPECIFIC SCOPE

Study Scope & Deliverables

The scope of each traffic study is tailored to the project. Depending on requirements, deliverables may include:

  • Traffic survey data and summary tables
  • Traffic composition and peak-hour analysis
  • Traffic flow and movement analysis
  • PCU calculations where applicable
  • Capacity and operational assessment
  • Traffic impact findings
  • Access and circulation observations
  • Engineering recommendations
  • Technical report and supporting figures
