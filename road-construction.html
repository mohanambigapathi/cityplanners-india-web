Road Construction Consultancy Services

CityPlanners provides consultancy support for road and infrastructure projects, helping clients with construction planning, coordination, monitoring, technical review and project delivery.

Construction Planning

Construction sequencing, activity planning, work schedules, resource planning and coordination of project activities.

Project Management

Project coordination, progress monitoring, work planning, contractor coordination and project execution support.

Site Supervision Support

Site progress monitoring, work inspection coordination, construction activities review and reporting.

Quality Control Support

Review of construction quality procedures, inspection records, testing coordination and compliance documentation.

Road Construction Coordination

Coordination of road construction activities, utilities, drainage, pavement works and associated infrastructure.

Progress Monitoring

Tracking project progress, identifying delays, monitoring milestones and preparing progress reports.

Our Project Support Approach

01. Project Understanding
